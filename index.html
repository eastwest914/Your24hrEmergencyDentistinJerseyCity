Emergency Dentist Answers

What should I do if my tooth is knocked out?

Pick the tooth up by the crown, not the root, rinse it gently without scrubbing and call us immediately. Getting seen quickly gives the tooth the best chance.


Is there a 24hr dentist near me in Jersey City?

Yes. Our office on John F. Kennedy Blvd is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.


What if my crown or filling falls out?

Call us. A missing crown or filling can leave the tooth sensitive and exposed, and we can examine it and repair it or protect it until a permanent fix.


Do you handle emergency extractions and root canals?

Yes. We will examine the tooth and discuss whether it can be saved with a root canal or needs to be removed.
